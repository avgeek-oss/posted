A draft keeps an idea without creating delivery jobs. Use **Save as draft** in the composer whenever you want to keep working later. You can save an empty post, no selected accounts, disconnected accounts or text that exceeds a provider limit.

The draft list shows your team's saved work, with the selected accounts and last saved time. Open a post title to continue editing.

## Save and reopen

1. Start from **Dashboard** or **Create post** and write the master text. Use **Add Another Post** or **Insert post** to build a sequence.
2. Choose accounts, then customize text and media in an account's **Edit** view when needed. Save as draft preserves the complete sequence, selected accounts, master media and each account's overrides and attachment order.
3. Open **Pending Drafts** under Posts. Click the post title or its edit action to reopen it. The summary reports the number of posts and accounts.
4. Make changes and choose **Save as draft** again. **Drafted by** identifies the last person who successfully saved it; **Last saved** follows your date, time and time-zone preferences.

The sidebar badge counts saved unpublished drafts across the team. Changes in the account chooser remain pending until **Choose X accounts**. Closing that dialog leaves the saved selection unchanged.

## Publish a draft

Review each selected account in **Preview**, resolve its content errors, then choose **Post in X accounts** for immediate publication or **Schedule for later** from its menu. Both paths ask you to review the destinations before confirming. Publishing converts the whole draft into one delivery set; it is no longer a pending draft after acceptance.

If validation fails, the draft remains available. A stale browser cannot overwrite another person's newer save. Repeating a logical save or publication with its original request key does not create another draft or delivery set. If a response is lost, use the displayed retry action before changing the content.

## Reset and remove content

**Reset to master** asks for confirmation before discarding an account post's custom text and media. Removing a master post also asks for confirmation and removes that post's account overrides. Removing an account through the chooser asks for confirmation when custom content would be lost.

Administrators and members can edit and delete drafts. Viewers can inspect them. Deleting a draft requires confirmation and does not cancel a delivery set that was already created from it. Use [Scheduled Posts](/scheduling) to cancel pending delivery instead.
